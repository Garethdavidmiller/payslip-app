🏖️ Holiday Pay Premium Estimate

Your variable pay × 7.69% — paid each January

HPP is a lump sum Chiltern pay each January. When you take annual leave you only receive your base rate — HPP compensates for the overtime and rest day pay you missed out on.

Estimated HPP for current year's data
£–
Enter hours across your periods above to calculate
Formula (confirmed by Chiltern payroll): Total variable pay (overtime + rest day hours + London Allowance) × 4/52 = 7.69%. Excludes basic pay, peer training, expenses and bonuses. This estimate covers the tax year 2025/26 — Chiltern will pay it in January 2027. Pro-rated if you were not employed for the full year. The amount is not in dispute; only the 8–21 month lag in payment timing.
